# About

## About me

I'm Archana, a Cyber Security graduate from RMIT University, building my knowledge and practical skills in Identity and Access Management (IAM) and Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC).

I'm particularly interested in the questions behind identity and access decisions: Who or what is requesting access? What should they be allowed to do? Who is accountable for that decision, and how can we demonstrate that it is appropriate?

I learn by understanding how things work and applying that knowledge. Each topic on this site brings together three perspectives: technical notes explaining how identity systems work, governance notes exploring how organisations manage and oversee them, and practical labs where I apply both to realistic organisational scenarios.

I'm building this portfolio to document my learning, develop practical skills and work towards a role as an IAM Analyst or IAM Governance Analyst.
