# Home

## About me

I'm Archana, a Master of Cyber Security graduate from RMIT University, building towards a career in Identity and Access Management (IAM) and Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC).

I'm particularly interested in how organisations manage digital identities, make access decisions and establish accountability around them. I'm working towards an IAM Analyst or IAM Governance Analyst role.

## What I'm building

This website is where I document my learning and build practical knowledge of IAM and its governance. Each topic brings together technical notes on how identity systems work, governance perspectives on how organisations manage them, and practical labs where I apply what I've learned to realistic scenarios.

I'm using this space to connect concepts with real organisational challenges, track my progress and build a portfolio that demonstrates how I approach IAM from both technical and governance perspectives.
